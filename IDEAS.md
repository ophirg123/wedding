# Invitation — idea bank

Written after three rejected attempts at redrawing the timeline icons (plant
characters, abstract vines, watercolour+gold) and one rejected motion idea
(the growing vine spine). The lesson recorded: **more botanical ornament on an
already-botanical page makes it busier, not better.** Ideas below are grouped by
what they actually change.

Assets we own and under-use: the wax-seal logo, the propagation concept
(cuttings → roots → merged root mass), Michal = pothos / Ophir = monstera,
the favour cuttings in glass bottles, the photo booth, the bus sheets,
the he / nl / dati variants, 325+ guests in a live Google Sheet.

---

## A. The opening moment (one-time, not scroll decoration)

1. **Rooting, not loading.** The page opens on a bare cutting in water. While
   assets load, roots draw downward; when they reach full length the invitation
   rises into place. The loading bar *is* the propagation. Nothing else on the
   internet does this, and it is literally the brand.
2. **Wax seal break.** The card arrives sealed with the logo seal; one tap and
   the seal cracks and the card unfolds. Uses an asset already approved for the
   favour tags. Must be skippable and once-per-device.
3. ~~**Addressed envelope.**~~ REJECTED - no per-guest links. With `?g=`, the envelope is addressed to the guest in
   Hebrew calligraphy before it opens.

## B. Hero

4. **Parallax depth** — the background split into 2–3 layers (far wash, mid
   leaves, near leaves) drifting at different speeds. Requires splitting
   `g_bg.png`; subtle, no new artwork.
5. **The page knows it is an evening wedding.** After dark in the guest's local
   time the palette deepens, gold warms. Same design, different hour.
6. **Countdown that speaks** — months → weeks → "מחר" → "היום".

## C. The propagation thread

7. **Roots instead of leaves.** Each timeline moment sends a root downward;
   at 02:00 (מעגל סיום) all six roots have merged into the logo's root mass.
   The evening literally roots. (Note: the *leaf* version of this was rejected —
   roots are the part of the mark nobody has used yet.)
8. **Waterline as scroll progress** — a hairline that rises as you read, ending
   at the waterline of the mark.

## D. Substance the page is missing (the real gap)

9. **RSVP** — matched against the guest sheet: attending, how many, bus seat,
   dietary, kids. The page is currently a poster, not a tool.
10. **Bus registration** — the two sheets already exist (הלוך / חזור).
11. ~~**Personalisation** — `?g=name`, `?side=bride|groom`~~ REJECTED - no per-guest
    links. `?lang` and `?dati` stay (they are variants, not per-guest).
    Replaces the separate `nl/` and `dati/` files with one page.
12. **Practical answers** — parking, it is a forest in November (bring a layer),
    heels on grass, kids, when it really ends, gift/Bit.
13. **A photo of the couple.** There is not one anywhere on the page.
14. **Day-of mode** — the current moment highlights itself; "החופה בעוד 10 דקות".
15. **After the wedding** — the same URL becomes thank-you + the album, so the
    link everyone already has keeps working.

## E. Brand-true delight

16. **The cutting you take home.** Explain the favour, with care instructions.
    A QR on the tag → a care page (light, water, when to pot it). Guests keep a
    living piece of the wedding; the page keeps serving after the day.
17. **The mark that ages.** BRIEF.md already says the logo matures over the
    years — an anniversary page where it grows.
18. **Easter egg** — tap the logo and its roots grow.
19. **Two small marks by the names** — a pothos leaf by מיכל, a monstera by
    אופיר. One glyph each, not a scene.

## Judgement

- Biggest *delight* per unit of risk: **1 (rooting) and 16 (the cutting)**.
- Biggest *value*: **9** (RSVP). Personalisation (11) is rejected. — turns a poster into
  the thing 325 guests actually need.
- Biggest *embarrassment risk if ignored*: **20 and 21**.
- Rejected, do not retry: redrawing the timeline icons; decorative vines and
  leaves added to the timeline; tinting the icon linework.
